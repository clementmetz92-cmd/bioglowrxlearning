How to study with it

  1. Open Explore the table and tap a few elements to get a feel for the families.
  2. Play Name → Symbol and Symbol → Name until you score 70% or more.
  3. Then try Find it on the table and Which family? to learn where each element sits.

Questions

Can I learn just the first part of the table?

Yes. Pick "First 54" for elements 1 to 54, then switch to "All 118" when you're ready.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Your scores, streaks and certificates are saved on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers.
